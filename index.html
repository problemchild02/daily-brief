CCI Opens Market Study into Quick Commerce Sector Amid Antitrust Concerns

The competition watchdog is examining exclusive arrangements between platforms and dark-store operators as grocery delivery consolidates rapidly.

The Competition Commission of India announced a suo motu market study into the quick commerce sector, citing concerns over exclusive supply arrangements and potential barriers to entry for smaller players. The study will examine platforms including Blinkit, Zepto, and Instamart, with preliminary findings expected within six months.

Why it matters

Reliance Retail's JioMart and associated quick-commerce operations could fall within the study's scope. Any interim CCI guidance on exclusivity clauses may affect ongoing supplier negotiations and dark-store partnership terms.

DPDPA Rules Gazette Notification Expected Before November Deadline

MeitY is finalising the rules package; consent manager obligations trigger in November 2026.

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology is expected to gazette the final Data Protection Rules before the November 13 deadline, when consent manager provisions take effect. Organisations face full compliance by May 2027, covering consent notices, privacy policies, and security safeguards.

Why it matters

Reliance Retail handles customer data across physical retail, JioMart, and loyalty programmes. Consent architecture and privacy notices must be operational well before the May 2027 deadline — preparation should begin now.

NCLAT Upholds CCI's ₹213 Crore Fine on Meta for WhatsApp Data-Sharing Policy

Appellate tribunal confirms the penalty but reverses the five-year data-sharing ban.

The National Company Law Appellate Tribunal upheld the Competition Commission's ₹213 crore penalty against Meta and WhatsApp for abusing WhatsApp's dominant position by compelling users to accept cross-platform data sharing. The tribunal reversed the CCI's additional order banning data sharing between WhatsApp and Facebook for five years, finding it disproportionate.

Why it matters

The ruling confirms that dominant digital platforms face antitrust liability for bundled data-sharing mandates — a precedent relevant to any Reliance platform products that share user data across group entities.

Supreme Court Reaffirms Kompetenz-Kompetenz Principle in Standard-Form Arbitration Clauses

A five-judge bench rules that arbitral tribunals may determine their own jurisdiction even where arbitrability is disputed at the referral stage.

The Supreme Court's constitution bench held that courts at the Section 11 stage should adopt a prima facie standard and refer disputes to arbitration unless the arbitration clause is manifestly null and void. The ruling harmonises conflicting High Court decisions on the scope of judicial intervention before arbitration begins.

Why it matters

Standard-form supplier and vendor agreements at Reliance Retail typically include arbitration clauses. This ruling reduces the risk of courts rejecting referrals at threshold — reducing litigation risk and shortening the path to dispute resolution.
